    How MeasureQuest works

    ⚡ Earn XP

    10–20 XP per correct answer depending on difficulty, +5 for answering in under 10 s, and a streak bonus. Repeating a question already answered correctly earns reduced XP. Labs and challenges give 10–40 XP.

    🎖️ Climb the ranks

    Trainee → Lab Assistant → Technician → Calibration Engineer → Test Engineer → Instrumentation Engineer → Metrologist → Chief Measurement Officer.

    🏆 Play fair

    Only Section C USNs can register. Each student signs in with a personal 4-digit PIN; five wrong PINs lock the account for 15 minutes. The leaderboard is visible only to signed-in students and shows nicknames and the last three USN digits. Leaderboard XP is checked by the server with a daily cap. Answers and response times are recorded for course improvement and outcome assessment.
